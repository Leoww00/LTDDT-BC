CAN TIN KTX - JAVA OOP WEB - DANG NHAP + NAP VI GIA LAP
======================================================
1. Giai nen file ZIP. Mo thu muc DatDoAnKTX_Web_Java_DangNhap_NapVi.
2. Nhan dup MO_WEBSITE.bat (Windows). Can cai JDK 17 tro len.
3. Neu mo bang IntelliJ: mo dung thu muc nay; chay src/main/Main.java.
4. Trinh duyet: http://localhost:9090 (neu 9090 ban se tu thu cong khac).
5. Tai khoan de demo:
      SV001 / 123456 (200.000d ban dau)
      SV002 / 123456 (80.000d ban dau)
6. Sau khi dang nhap, bam '+ Nap vi' -> nhap so tien -> Tao yeu cau
   -> bam 'Mo phong ngan hang xac nhan da chuyen'.
   LUC NAY Java moi cong tien vao vi. Day KHONG phai chuyen khoan that.
7. So du va lich su nap vi luu o data/student_wallets.tsv (khong xoa
   neu muon giu so du), mat khau luu bang ma bam PBKDF2 va muoi rieng.
8. Muon reset du lieu ve ban dau: tat website, xoa file data/student_wallets.tsv,
   chay lai. Bai nay chi la demo tren localhost.
9. Dat mon: chon mon -> them vao gio -> thanh toan vi hoac tien mat
   hoac chuyen khoan (hai hinh thuc sau la mo phong cho thanh toan).
10. Da co Java OOP: Dong goi, ke thua, da hinh, truu tuong, interface,
    ArrayList, Map, try-catch, File IO; 5 package: model, service,
    repository, utils, main.

LUU Y AN TOAN:
- KHONG su dung mat khau ngan hang, OTP, thong tin the that tren demo.
- Khong duoc cong tien vao vi that dua vao nut 'Da chuyen tien'.
- Neu muon nap tien that, can dang ky nha cung cap thanh toan co API,
  xac minh thong bao thanh toan tu server va chong lap giao dich.
- Ban demo chi chay local, chua co hoan thien bao mat de trien khai cong khai.
